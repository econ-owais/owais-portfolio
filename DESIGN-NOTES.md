# FIELDWORK — design direction

A custom editorial identity for Owais Ali Shah’s work at the intersection of economics, data and public purpose.

Oversized Space Grotesk lettering anchors the portrait opening. Cormorant Garamond supplies an academic editorial voice. Mineral charcoal, warm cream, pale sage and rust form the core palette. The original portrait is presented through CSS, with its source pixels unchanged.

Custom abstract contour illustrations connect climate exposure and economic systems. They are explicitly labeled as abstract project art, not dashboard screenshots. Each project has an individual title treatment and visual composition.

Thematic research discovery, visitor-specific routes, previews and comparison help audiences navigate the full archive. The synthetic signal lab makes analytical thinking tangible without inventing research findings. Existing search, themes, CV, credentials and content remain available.

The layout responds down to 320 CSS pixels. Native controls, semantic headings, reduced-motion support and standard page scrolling preserve usability. This is a static manual-upload edition with no application backend or build step.
