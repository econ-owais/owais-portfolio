#### KPK Flood Impact Dashboard — Premium → project-floodv2.html

Interactive flood analytics across all 35 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, 2010–2025. 130 curated data points from PDMA KP, NDMA, and OCHA. Features: interactive Leaflet choropleth map with click-to-filter, district drill-down profiles with severity index, animated timeline, two-year comparison, district × year heatmap, bilingual EN/UR with RTL, dark mode, animated hero with particles, sparklines in every KPI card, and 8 analytical tabs.

Stack: R · Shiny · Leaflet · Bootstrap 5 · highcharter · reactable · sf · Bilingual · 130 data points · 35 districts · 2010–2025

Reference: https://econ-owaisali.shinyapps.io/kpk_flood_dashboard_v2/

Problem: District-level flood exposure across KP was scattered across PDMA, NDMA, and OCHA sources — no single view supported planning comparisons.

Approach: Harmonized 130 data points across 35 districts and 15 years (2010–2025). Built an R Shiny app with a Leaflet choropleth, click-to-filter map, drill-down profiles, and a composite severity index.

Output: Eight analytical tabs including timeline animation, two-year comparison, district × year heatmap, and bilingual EN/UR interface with RTL support.

Impact: Enables rapid comparison of flood exposure across districts — designed for planning and research audiences.

#### WDI Panel Data Dashboard → project-wdi.html

Interactive FDI inflows, macroeconomic indicators, panel regression results, World Development Indicators, two-way fixed effects, robust standard errors, and cross-country comparisons.

Stack: Streamlit · Python · WDI · FDI · Panel Data · TWFE · Robust SE

Reference: https://owais-wdi-panel-dashboard.streamlit.app/

Problem: FDI–growth literature rarely ships with a reproducible, interactive panel pipeline.

Approach: Automated WDI ingestion with Python + Streamlit. Panel OLS with two-way fixed effects (country + year) and robust standard errors.

Output: Interactive FDI inflow charts, macro indicators, and cross-country comparison views.

Impact: Reusable template for panel workflows; reduces a fresh analysis from days to hours.
